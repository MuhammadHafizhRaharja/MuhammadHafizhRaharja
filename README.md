## About

<img align="right" width="140" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b206f421-052b-4444-bcdb-48c41c85c18b" alt="Chibi character in an animal onesie typing at a laptop"/>

I study Information Systems (D3) at Telkom University in Bandung. Most of what I build is IoT end to end: ESP32-C3 firmware, the cloud API it reports to, and the mobile app on the other side. More and more of it involves LLMs too, from RAG over office documents to text-to-speech for a robot.

Right now I'm building **MomotBot**, an AI companion robot, and **DocuMind**, a document AI platform.
